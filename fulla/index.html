Fulla is a personal manager agent that runs on Norns.

You interact with Fulla on Telegram, Signal or SMS. Given access to your calendar, your reminders, and your messages, it can help you track commitments, draft responses, and manage your time. Norns, the orchestrator for long-lived agents, allows workflows that span hours, days, or months.

Structure

What runs where

Norns holds the conversation and every sleeping reminder. It is the only process that is always on; the worker that runs the model and the tools is stopped most of the day.

managerone conversation

Chat, the morning brief and the nightly review share one conversation, so Fulla remembers what was said earlier in the day.

reminderone run each

Each reminder is its own run. It sleeps until due, asks, and closes when you say done.

workerserverless

Norns wakes the worker when a run needs a tool, and it stops when idle. A reminder a week out costs nothing until it is due.

machinesyour devices

Small workers on your Mac and a Linux box give Fulla Things, Reminders, iMessage drafts and a terminal. If the Mac is asleep, the task waits until it reconnects.

readersub-agent

Web pages are read by a separate agent that can search and fetch but cannot act.

friendsand groups

People you allow talk to a separate agent that has none of your facts, calendar, reminders or machines.

Run it

Against a local orchestrator

Locally Fulla is a normal Norns worker that stays up and long-polls Telegram. It needs a local orchestrator, an Anthropic key, a bot token from @BotFather and your numeric Telegram id.
